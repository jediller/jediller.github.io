Today's state is Delaware.

Delaware is a small Mid-Atlantic state on the eastern side of the Delmarva Peninsula. It borders Maryland to the west and south, Pennsylvania to the north, and New Jersey across the Delaware River and Delaware Bay. Most of the state lies on the low, nearly level Atlantic Coastal Plain, with sandy soils, wetlands, tidal creeks, and beaches along the ocean and bay. A narrow section of rolling Piedmont reaches northern Delaware near Wilmington. The state's highest natural point, near Ebright Azimuth, is only about 448 feet above sea level. The Delaware River, Christina River, Brandywine Creek, and broad Delaware Bay connect the state to the Atlantic. Wilmington and much of the population cluster in the north, close to Philadelphia and major transportation routes. Farther south, flat fertile land supports corn, soybeans, and a large poultry industry. Coastal resorts benefit from beaches, while low elevation leaves shore communities and wetlands exposed to flooding, erosion, and sea-level rise.

Andorra is a small landlocked country high in the eastern Pyrenees between France and Spain. Steep mountains fill nearly all of its territory, and narrow valleys hold most roads, farms, and settlements. Coma Pedrosa, the highest peak, rises to about 2,942 meters, or 9,652 feet. The Gran Valira River forms where mountain streams meet near the capital, Andorra la Vella, then flows south into Spain and eventually joins the Segre River. Winters are cold and snowy at higher elevations, while summers are generally mild; rainfall and snow vary sharply with altitude and slope. Forests cover many lower slopes, and alpine meadows lie above the tree line. Limited level land concentrates the population in the main valley and makes construction costly. Mountain scenery and dependable winter snow support ski resorts, hiking, and tourism, while the country's location between two larger neighbors has encouraged cross-border trade and shopping. Its rugged terrain has also helped preserve a distinct Catalan-speaking culture.
