Today is Sunday, August ninth, two thousand twenty-six. Here is what the movers and shakers are noted as saying in the last twenty-four hours.

No impactful new statement from the twenty-one people on the standing watch list could be verified within the rolling period, so none is attributed to them. The most consequential verified American statements instead came from two senior federal officials.

Pentagon spokesman Sean Parnell said the Defense Department was actively working to accelerate munitions purchases and provide weapons at what he called the pace the threat demands. He confirmed that defense companies had been asked for much faster production and delivery plans. Parnell said the effort would inform the fiscal year twenty twenty-eight budget and described it as part of a broader push to rebuild the defense industrial base, not solely a reaction to weapons used in the Iran war.

Acting Immigration and Customs Enforcement Director David Venturella said the agency was committed to transparency and accountability and expected every officer and agent in the field to have a body-worn camera by the end of August. Responding to questions about when footage may be released, he said withholding or delaying video can protect investigations and privacy. He also said the agency's policy had been mischaracterized and argued that its restrictions were consistent with federal law and other federal law-enforcement practices.
