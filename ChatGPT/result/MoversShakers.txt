Today is Saturday, August eighth, two thousand twenty-six. Here is what the movers and shakers are noted as saying in the last twenty-four hours.

President Donald Trump told a State Department gathering of mining executives that the United States was reclaiming what he called its rightful place as the minerals superpower of the world. He said critical minerals power products ranging from advanced weapons to automobiles and should be mined, refined, and made in the United States. His administration announced about three billion dollars in loans and other support for critical-mineral, battery, and mining-education projects.

Trump also responded to a federal appeals court order halting aboveground construction of his planned White House ballroom unless Congress approves it. He called the decision unjust and said his administration would immediately appeal to the Supreme Court.

Senator Charles Schumer urged the National Park Service to release congressionally approved operating money for the Theodore Roosevelt Inaugural National Historic Site in Buffalo. He said the delayed funds were needed to pay staff, bills, and essential repairs at the only national park site that had not yet received its annual operating support.

After the Senate confirmed Todd Blanche as attorney general, Blanche said he was deeply honored by Trump’s trust and grateful to the senators who supported him. Republican Senator Bill Cassidy, explaining his vote for confirmation, said Blanche was not perfect, but no one is, and argued that a different nominee might not be better.
