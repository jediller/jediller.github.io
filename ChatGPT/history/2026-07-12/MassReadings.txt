Fifteenth Sunday in Ordinary Time, Lectionary 103.

The first reading is Isaiah 55:10-11. God’s word is compared with rain and snow that water the earth, produce seed and bread, and accomplish the purpose for which they were sent.

The responsorial psalm is Psalm 65:10-14, praising God for watering the land and crowning the year with abundance.

The second reading is Romans 8:18-23. Paul places present suffering within a larger hope as creation and humanity await freedom and redemption.

The Gospel is Matthew 13:1-23, with a shorter form ending at verse 9. Jesus tells the parable of seed falling on different soils and explains that fruitful hearing requires an open, enduring heart.

The readings picture God’s word as generous and effective while asking what kind of soil listeners will become.
