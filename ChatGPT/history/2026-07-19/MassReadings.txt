Sixteenth Sunday in Ordinary Time, Lectionary 106.

The first reading is Wisdom 12:13, 16-19. God’s power is shown through justice, patience, and leniency, teaching that those who are righteous must also be kind and leave room for repentance.

The responsorial psalm is Psalm 86:5-6, 9-10, and 15-16. The assembly praises God as good, forgiving, merciful, and attentive to prayer.

The second reading is Romans 8:26-27. Paul says the Spirit helps human weakness and intercedes when believers do not know how to pray.

The Gospel is Matthew 13:24-43, with a shorter option ending at verse 30. Jesus tells the parables of weeds among wheat, the mustard seed, and yeast. God’s kingdom grows quietly, and final judgment belongs to God rather than impatient human hands.

The readings join patience, hope, prayer, and trust in God’s final justice.
