Tennessee stretches from the Appalachian Mountains in the east to the Mississippi River in the west. Nashville is the capital and a major music and health-care center; Memphis is known for blues, soul, logistics, and its Mississippi River location. The state’s three broad regions are reflected in the three stars on its flag.

Vietnam is a long, narrow country on the eastern side of mainland Southeast Asia. Hanoi is the capital, while Ho Chi Minh City is its largest commercial center. Mountains line much of the western interior, and the Red River and Mekong deltas support dense populations and extensive rice farming. Its long coast faces the South China Sea.
