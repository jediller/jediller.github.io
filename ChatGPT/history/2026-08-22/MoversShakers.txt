Today is Saturday, August 22, 2026. Here is what the movers and shakers are noted as saying in the last 24 hours.

President Donald Trump used a campaign rally in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina, to urge Republicans to support Senator Darline Graham in Tuesday's runoff against Representative Ralph Norman. Trump told the crowd that although he is not personally running in the midterms, he and the country are effectively on the ballot, and he pressed supporters to turn out for Graham. He defended her after criticism of her limited foreign policy experience, saying she had been honest and would learn the issues.

Trump also addressed his administration's temporary plan to permit three hundred thousand metric tons of beef imports over ninety days at a lower tariff. Responding to objections from cattle producers and rural Republicans, he said the added supply would bring consumer prices down a little and insisted that ranchers themselves recognize the need for help on prices. He did not identify the countries expected to provide the beef, but said they would send high-quality products.

After Chief Justice John Roberts temporarily allowed construction of the White House ballroom project to continue, Trump said he was grateful for the decision. In a social media post and at the rally, he described the planned military and ballroom complex as vital to national security and predicted that it would be the greatest facility of its kind.

On economic policy, Trump told reporters he had not directed Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent to intervene in the long-term government bond market. He said Bessent acted on his own authority, praised him as capable, and endorsed the decision after the fact. At the South Carolina rally, Trump also defended his pressure campaign against Iran and portrayed American control of the Strait of Hormuz as necessary to protect navigation and commerce.
