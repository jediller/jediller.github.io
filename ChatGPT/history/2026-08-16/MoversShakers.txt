Today is Sunday, August sixteenth, two thousand twenty-six. Here is what the movers and shakers are noted as saying in the last twenty-four hours.

President Donald Trump used a Saturday post on Truth Social to attack members of the National Trust for Historic Preservation over litigation concerning his planned White House ballroom. Trump called the trust's members treasonists and alleged that the lawsuit revealed military secrets about a project he described as a military complex. He argued that the ballroom project is vital to national security and to the safety of presidents, their families, White House staff and Cabinet members. Those were Trump's claims in his own social media statement; the post did not establish that the disputed construction details were classified.

Hawaii Governor Josh Green urged residents to stay home and off the roads while Hurricane Lala passed south of the Big Island. He called it an enormous storm and reported that one person had died in a traffic crash, nineteen roofs had been lost and one hundred ninety flights had been postponed. His warning emphasized the continuing danger from flooding, mudslides, powerful surf and power outages even though the storm did not make landfall.

Democratic National Committee Chairman Ken Martin defended the party's newly approved early-state calendar for the two thousand twenty-eight presidential nomination. In comments reported after the committee's Saturday vote, Martin said candidates should be battle-tested and should have to campaign for support across diverse communities. The plan places South Carolina first on January twenty-second, followed by Nevada on February first, then New Hampshire, New Mexico, Michigan and Virginia.
