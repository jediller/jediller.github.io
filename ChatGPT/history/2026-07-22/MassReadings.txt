Feast of Saint Mary Magdalene, Lectionary 603.

The first reading may be Song of Songs 3:1-4b, in which the beloved searches through the city and finally finds the one her heart loves, or 2 Corinthians 5:14-17, where Paul says Christ’s love impels believers into a new creation.

The responsorial psalm is Psalm 63:2, 3-4, 5-6, and 8-9. The soul thirsts for God and finds joyful security under God’s protection.

The Gospel is John 20:1-2 and 11-18. Mary Magdalene comes to the tomb, remains there in grief, recognizes the risen Jesus when he calls her by name, and is sent to announce the resurrection to the disciples.

The feast presents persistent love transformed into recognition, mission, and hope.
