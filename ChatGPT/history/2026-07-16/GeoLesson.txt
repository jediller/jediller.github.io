Hawaii is the only United States state composed entirely of islands and the only one located in the tropics. Its volcanic chain lies in the central Pacific, far southwest of the continental states. Honolulu, on Oahu, is the capital and largest urban area. Tourism, defense, astronomy, agriculture, and ocean research are important, while active volcanoes continue to shape the landscape.

Switzerland is a landlocked country in central Europe bordered by France, Germany, Austria, Liechtenstein, and Italy. The Alps dominate the south and center, while much of the population lives on the Swiss Plateau. Bern is the federal city; Zurich is the largest city and an international financial center. The country has four national languages: German, French, Italian, and Romansh.
