Monday of the Sixteenth Week in Ordinary Time, Lectionary 395.

The first reading is Micah 6:1-4 and 6-8. God recalls rescuing Israel, and the prophet rejects extravagant offerings as substitutes for faithful living. What is required is to do justice, love goodness, and walk humbly with God.

The responsorial psalm is Psalm 50:5-6, 8-9, 16bc-17, 21, and 23. God does not need empty sacrifice; genuine praise and an upright life honor him.

The Gospel is Matthew 12:38-42. When some ask Jesus for a sign, he points to Jonah and to the wisdom recognized by the queen of the south. The call is to respond to the truth already present rather than continually demanding proof.

The readings emphasize justice, humility, attentive conversion, and worship expressed through life.
