Wednesday July 29, 2026.

Memorial of Saints Martha, Mary, and Lazarus.

First Reading.
Jeremiah, chapter 15, verse 10, and verses 16 through 21.

A reading from the Book of the Prophet Jeremiah.

Woe is me, my mother:
why hast thou borne me a man of brawling,
a man of discord in all the earth?
I have not lent to usury,
neither hath any man lent unto me to usury:
all curse me.

Thy words were found, and I did eat them,
and thy word was made to me a joy
and gladness of my heart:
because thy name is invoked upon me,
O Lord God of hosts.

I sat not in the council of jesters,
and I have gloried at the face of thy hand:
I sat alone,
because thou hast filled me with threatening.

Why is my sorrow made perpetual,
and my desperate plague refuseth to be cured?
It is become unto me
as a lie of unfaithful waters.

For this cause thus saith our Lord:
If thou wilt be converted, I will convert thee,
and thou shalt stand before my face:
and if thou wilt separate the precious thing from the vile,
thou shalt be as my mouth:
they shall be turned to thee,
and thou shalt not be turned to them.

And I will give thee unto this people
as a brazen wall, strong:
and they shall fight against thee,
and shall not prevail:
because I am with thee to save thee,
and to deliver thee, saith our Lord.

And I will deliver thee
out of the hand of the most wicked,
and I will redeem thee
out of the hand of the strong.

The word of the Lord.

Thanks be to God.

Responsorial Psalm.
Psalm 59, verses 2, 3, 4, 10, 11, 17, and 18.

The response is: God is my refuge on the day of distress.

Deliver me from mine enemies, O my God:
and from them that rise up against me defend me.
Deliver me from them that work iniquity:
and from bloody men save me.

The response is: God is my refuge on the day of distress.

Because lo they have taken my soul:
the strong have fallen violently upon me.

The response is: God is my refuge on the day of distress.

I will keep my strength to thee:
because thou art my receiver:
my God, thy mercy shall prevent me.
God will show unto me concerning mine enemies.

The response is: God is my refuge on the day of distress.

But I will sing thy strength:
and will exalt thy mercy in the morning.
Because thou art become my receiver,
and my refuge in the day of my tribulation.

The response is: God is my refuge on the day of distress.

My helper, I will sing to thee,
because thou art God my receiver:
my God, my mercy.

The response is: God is my refuge on the day of distress.

Gospel Acclamation.

Alleluia, alleluia.

I am the light of the world:
he that followeth me walketh not in darkness,
but shall have the light of life.

Alleluia, alleluia.

The Lord be with you.

And with your spirit.

A reading from the holy Gospel according to John.

Glory to you, O Lord.

John, chapter 11, verses 19 through 27.

Many of the Jews were come to Martha and Mary,
to comfort them concerning their brother.
Martha therefore, when she heard that Jesus was come,
went to meet him;
but Mary sat at home.

Martha therefore said to Jesus:
Lord, if thou hadst been here,
my brother had not died.
But now also I know
that what things soever thou shalt ask of God,
God will give thee.

Jesus saith to her:
Thy brother shall rise again.

Martha saith to him:
I know that he shall rise again
in the resurrection, in the last day.

Jesus saith to her:
I am the resurrection and the life;
he that believeth in me,
although he be dead, shall live.
And every one that liveth, and believeth in me,
shall not die for ever.
Believest thou this?

She saith to him:
Yea, Lord,
I have believed that thou art Christ,
the Son of God that art come into this world.

The Gospel of the Lord.

Praise to you, Lord Jesus Christ.
