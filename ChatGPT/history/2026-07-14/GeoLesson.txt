Minnesota is a northern United States state bordering Canada and Lake Superior. It is called the Land of 10,000 Lakes, although it has even more. The Twin Cities of Minneapolis and Saint Paul form its largest metropolitan area. Agriculture, medical technology, manufacturing, finance, and food companies are important to its economy. The Mississippi River begins at Lake Itasca in northern Minnesota.

Peru lies on the Pacific coast of western South America. Its landscape rises from a dry coastal plain through the Andes Mountains and descends into the Amazon basin. Lima is the capital and largest city. Peru was the center of the Inca Empire, and Machu Picchu remains one of its best-known archaeological sites. Spanish is the most widely spoken language, while Quechua, Aymara, and other Indigenous languages also have official recognition in the places where they predominate.
