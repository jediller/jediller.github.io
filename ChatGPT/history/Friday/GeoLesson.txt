Today’s United States location is Wisconsin. Wisconsin lies between Lake Superior and Lake Michigan and was heavily shaped by continental glaciers. Ice sheets left rolling moraines, thousands of lakes, and fertile deposits, while the southwestern Driftless Area escaped the most recent glaciation and retains steep valleys and ridges.
