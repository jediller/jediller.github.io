Saturday of the Fifteenth Week in Ordinary Time, Lectionary 394.

The first reading is Micah 2:1-5. The prophet condemns powerful people who plan wrongdoing, seize fields and houses, and deprive families of their inheritance. Their abuse of power will bring judgment and loss.

The responsorial psalm is Psalm 10:1-4, 7-8, and 14. The prayer asks why God seems distant when the wicked prey upon the vulnerable, yet it ends by entrusting the afflicted and the poor to God’s care.

The Gospel is Matthew 12:14-21. As opponents plot against him, Jesus withdraws and quietly heals those who follow. Matthew identifies him with Isaiah’s gentle servant, who does not crush the bruised reed and brings justice without self-promotion.

The readings contrast grasping power with Christ’s patient, protective strength.
