Market Closing Summary

United States stocks staged a dramatic intraday comeback before finishing mixed on Wednesday, November 25, 2026, as buyers stepped back in ahead of the Thanksgiving holiday to erase steep morning losses triggered by stubborn inflation data, fresh tariff threats, and early semiconductor weakness.

The Dow Jones Industrial Average erased an early drop of more than 1,100 points to finish up 164.25 points, or 0.33 percent, at 50,182.95, building on Tuesday's close of 50,018.70. After opening down at 49,469.86 and hitting an intraday low of 48,913.74, the blue-chip index rallied as high as 50,395.24 on volume of 754.71 million shares.

The S&P 500 recovered most of a 3 percent morning slide to close down just 18.31 points, or 0.25 percent, at 7,182.78 compared with Tuesday's finish of 7,201.09. Opening at 7,060.33, the broad market benchmark touched a low of 6,970.11 before climbing to 7,199.31 on volume of 3.47 billion shares.

The Nasdaq Composite pared a sharp early 4 percent plunge to finish down 147.73 points, or 0.62 percent, at 23,585.80 from Tuesday's close of 23,733.54. The tech-heavy index opened at 22,948.48, dropped to a low of 22,632.40, and rebounded to 23,627.45 on heavy volume of 9.76 billion shares.

From a broader historical perspective, all three benchmarks remain below their October 6, 2026 all-time record closes. Key Drivers, Yields & Commodities

Early selling on Wednesday followed news that the White House is preparing Section 301 tariffs on more than a dozen major trading partners, alongside the October Personal Consumption Expenditures report showing headline inflation accelerating to 3.8 percent year over year. That reading reinforced expectations for a December Federal Reserve interest rate hike. However, semiconductor shares pared steep morning losses and Anthropic reversed higher by roughly 1 percent, lifting the broader market into the close.

In bond and commodity markets, the benchmark 10-year United States Treasury yield eased to around 4.81 to 4.87 percent, touching its lowest level in roughly ten weeks. Meanwhile, Brent crude oil rose back above $91 per barrel on renewed Strait of Hormuz vessel attack reports. United States stock markets will be closed on Thursday for Thanksgiving and will reopen for a shortened session closing at 1:00 PM Eastern on Friday.
