Today's country is Iceland. Iceland is an island nation situated in the North Atlantic Ocean, just south of the Arctic Circle. Coastal fjords create natural harbors where towns like the capital, Reykjavik, developed. Isolation and ocean currents create a mild maritime climate relative to its northern latitude, supporting rich marine ecosystems that make commercial fishing and sustainable ecotourism key pillars of the national economy.
