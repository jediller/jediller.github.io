Tuesday August 18, 2026.


Tuesday of the Twentieth Week in Ordinary Time.


A reading from the Book of the Prophet Ezekiel.
The word of the Lord came to me: Son of man, say to the prince of Tyre: Thus says the Lord God: Because your heart has grown proud, and you say, A god am I! I sit in the seat of the gods in the heart of the sea! And yet you are a man, and not a god, though you think yourself as wise as a god. Look, you are wiser than Daniel, no secret is too obscure for you! By your wisdom and your intelligence you have made riches for yourself; you have put gold and silver into your treasuries. By your great wisdom in your commerce you have multiplied your riches, and your heart has grown proud because of your riches. Therefore thus says the Lord God: Because you think yourself as wise as a god, therefore look, I will bring against you foreigners, the most ruthless of nations; they shall draw their swords against your glorious wisdom, and defile your splendor. They shall thrust you down to the pit, and you shall die the death of the slain in the heart of the seas. Will you then say, I am a god, in the face of those who kill you? You are a man, and not a god, in the power of those who pierce you! You shall die the death of the uncircumcised by the hand of foreigners, for I have spoken, says the Lord God.
The word of the Lord. Thanks be to God.


Responsorial Psalm.
The response is: It is I who deal death and give life.


I would have said, I will make an end of them and blot out their name from men's memories, had I not feared the insolence of the enemy, feared that their foes would mistakenly boast, Our own hand won the victory; the Lord had nothing to do with it.
It is I who deal death and give life.


For they are a nation devoid of reason, having no understanding. If they had insight they would realize what happened, they would understand their future.
It is I who deal death and give life.


How could one man rout a thousand, or two put ten thousand to flight, unless their Rock had sold them, the Lord had delivered them up?
It is I who deal death and give life.


Close at hand is the day of their disaster, and their doom is rushing upon them! For the Lord will do justice for his people, and have compassion on his servants.
It is I who deal death and give life.


Alleluia.
Alleluia, alleluia. Jesus Christ became poor although he was rich, so that by his poverty you might become rich. Alleluia, alleluia.


A reading from the holy Gospel according to Matthew.
Glory to you, O Lord.


Jesus said to his disciples: Amen, I say to you, it will be hard for one who is rich to enter the Kingdom of heaven. Again I say to you, it is easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle than for one who is rich to enter the Kingdom of God. When the disciples heard this, they were greatly astonished and said, Who then can be saved? Jesus looked at them and said, For human beings this is impossible, but for God all things are possible. Then Peter said to him in reply, We have given up everything and followed you. What will there be for us? Jesus said to them, Amen, I say to you that you who have followed me, in the new age, when the Son of Man is seated on his throne of glory, will yourselves sit on twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel. And everyone who has given up houses or brothers or sisters or father or mother or children or lands for the sake of my name will receive a hundred times more, and will inherit eternal life. But many who are first will be last, and the last will be first.
The Gospel of the Lord. Praise to you, Lord Jesus Christ.
