Memorial of Saint Dominic, Priest.


A reading from the Book of the Prophet Habakkuk.
Are you not from eternity, O Lord, my holy God, immortal? O Lord, you have marked him for judgment, O Rock, you have readied him for punishment! Too pure are your eyes to look upon evil, and the sight of oppression you cannot endure. Why then do you gaze in silence upon the treacherous, while the wicked man devours one more just than himself? You have made man like the fish of the sea, like creeping things without a ruler. He brings them all up with his hook, he drags them away with his net, he gathers them in his seine; so he rejoices and is glad. Therefore he sacrifices to his net, and burns incense to his seine; for by them his portion is generous, and his sumptuously provided. Shall he then keep on emptying his net, and pitilessly slaying nations without end? I will stand at my guard post, and station myself upon the rampart, and keep watch to see what he will say to me, and what answer he will give to my complaint. Then the Lord answered me and said: Write down the vision clearly upon the tablets, so that one can read it readily. For the vision still has its time, presses on to fulfillment, and will not disappoint; if it delays, wait for it, it will surely come, it will not be late. The rash man has no integrity, but the just man, because of his faith, shall live.
The word of the Lord. Thanks be to God.


Responsorial Psalm.
The response is: The Lord will not abandon those who search for him.


The Lord sits enthroned forever; he has set up his throne for judgment. He judges the world with justice; he governs the peoples with equity.
The Lord will not abandon those who search for him.


The Lord is a stronghold for the oppressed, a stronghold in times of distress. Those who know your name trust in you, for you forsake not those who seek you, O Lord.
The Lord will not abandon those who search for him.


Sing praises to the Lord who dwells in Zion; declare his deeds among the nations. For he who avenges blood remembers them; he does not forget the cry of the afflicted.
The Lord will not abandon those who search for him.


Alleluia.
Alleluia, alleluia. Our Savior Jesus Christ has destroyed death and brought life to light through the Gospel. Alleluia, alleluia.


A reading from the holy Gospel according to Matthew.
Glory to you, O Lord.


A man came up to Jesus, knelt down before him, and said, Lord, have pity on my son, for he is a lunatic and suffers severely; often he falls into fire, and often into water. I brought him to your disciples, but they could not cure him. Jesus said in reply, O faithless and perverse generation, how long will I be with you? How long will I endure you? Bring him here to me. Jesus rebuked him and the demon came out of him, and from that hour the boy was cured. Then the disciples approached Jesus in private and said, Why could we not drive it out? He said to them, Because of your little faith. Amen, I say to you, if you have faith the size of a mustard seed, you will say to this mountain, Move from here to there, and it will move. Nothing will be impossible for you.
The Gospel of the Lord. Praise to you, Lord Jesus Christ.
