Market Status & Latest Closing Summary

United States financial markets are closed today, Thursday, November 26, 2026, in observance of the Thanksgiving Day holiday. Using the most recent official closing benchmarks from Wednesday, November 25, 2026, major U.S. stock indexes finished mixed after staging a dramatic intraday comeback that erased steep morning losses triggered by stubborn inflation data, fresh tariff proposals, and early semiconductor weakness.

Key Drivers, Yields & Commodities

Market-moving factors ahead of the holiday break centered on inflation, trade policy, and technology earnings sentiment. Wednesday's October Personal Consumption Expenditures report showed headline inflation accelerating to 3.8 percent year over year and core inflation rising 3.3 percent, slightly above forecasts, while third-quarter gross domestic product was revised up to 4.3 percent annualized growth. Those readings reinforced near-certain market expectations for a December Federal Reserve interest rate hike. Simultaneously, the Office of the United States Trade Representative proposed new Section 301 tariffs on 14 trading partners, including rates up to 35 percent on Vietnam, 25 percent on India, 20 percent on China, and 15 percent on the European Union and Japan, pending January hearings.
