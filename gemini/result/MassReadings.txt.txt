Sunday July 26, 2026.


Seventeenth Sunday in Ordinary Time.


A reading from the Book of Genesis.
In those days, the Lord said, The outcry against Sodom and Gomorrah is so great, and their sin so grave, that I must go down and see whether their actions fully correspond to the outcry that has reached me. Abraham remained standing before the Lord and approached him, asking, Will you sweep away the innocent with the guilty? Suppose there were fifty innocent people within the city; will you wipe out the place and not spare it for the sake of the fifty innocent people within it? Far be it from you to do such a thing! The Lord answered, If I find fifty innocent people in the city of Sodom, I will spare the whole place for their sake. Abraham spoke again: Suppose there are five less than fifty? Will you destroy the whole city for lack of five? He answered, I will not destroy it if I find forty-five there. Abraham persisted, speaking to the Lord again and again, lowering the count to forty, thirty, twenty, and finally ten. The Lord replied, For the sake of the ten, I will not destroy it.
The word of the Lord. Thanks be to God.


Responsorial Psalm.
The response is: Lord, on the day I called for help, you answered me.


I will give thanks to you, O Lord, with all my heart, for you have heard the words of my mouth; in the presence of the angels I will sing your praise; I will worship at your holy temple and give thanks to your name.
Lord, on the day I called for help, you answered me.


Though I walk in the midst of trouble, you preserve my life; against the anger of my enemies you stretch out your hand, and your right hand saves me. The Lord will fulfill his purpose for me; your love, O Lord, endures forever; do not abandon the work of your hands.
Lord, on the day I called for help, you answered me.


A reading from the Letter of Saint Paul to the Colossians.
Brothers and sisters: You were buried with Christ in baptism, in which you were also raised with him through faith in the working of God, who raised him from the dead. And even when you were dead in transgressions and the uncircumcision of your flesh, he brought you to life along with him, having forgiven us all our transgressions; obliterating the bond against us, with its legal claims, which was opposed to us, he also removed it from our midst, nailing it to the cross.
The word of the Lord. Thanks be to God.


Alleluia.
Alleluia, alleluia. You have received a Spirit of adoption as sons, through which we cry, Abba, Father! Alleluia, alleluia.


A reading from the holy Gospel according to Luke.
Glory to you, O Lord.


Jesus was praying in a certain place, and when he finished, one of his disciples said to him, Lord, teach us to pray just as John taught his disciples. He said to them, When you pray, say: Father, hallowed be your name, your kingdom come. Give us each day our daily bread and forgive us our sins for we ourselves forgive everyone in debt to us, and do not subject us to the final test. And he said to them, Suppose one of you has a friend to whom he goes at midnight and says, Friend, lend me three loaves of bread, for a friend of mine has arrived at my house from a journey and I have nothing to offer him, and he says in reply from within, Do not bother me; the door has already been locked and my children and I are already in bed. I tell you, if he does not get up to give the visitor the loaves because of their friendship, he will get up to give him whatever he needs because of his persistence. So I say to you, ask and it will be given to you; seek and you will find; knock and the door will be opened to you.
The Gospel of the Lord. Praise to you, Lord Jesus Christ.
