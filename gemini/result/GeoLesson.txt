Situated in the Great Plains region of the central United States, Nebraska is bounded by South Dakota to the north, Iowa and Missouri across the Missouri River to the east, Kansas to the south, Colorado to the southwest, and Wyoming to the west. Nebraska's geography forms the foundation of one of the nation's leading agricultural and food production economies. Nebraska ranks among the top states for beef cattle production and ethanol refining. Lincoln, located in the southeast, serves as the state capital and home to the flagship state university, while Omaha, situated on the Missouri River, acts as the state's largest city and a national hub for logistics, insurance, and rail transport.
