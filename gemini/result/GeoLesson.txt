Poland's central European plain geography has historically made it a strategic land bridge connecting Western and Eastern Europe. Its fertile central plains support extensive agriculture, including cereal grains and sugar beets, while rich coal, copper, and salt deposits in the southern Silesian region power heavy manufacturing and industrial exports. Poland's Baltic seaports, notably Gdansk and Gdynia, serve as major gateways for international maritime trade.
