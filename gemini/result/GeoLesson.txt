Washington, known as the Evergreen State, is located in the Pacific Northwest region of the United States. Covering approximately seventy-one thousand square miles, it is bordered by the Canadian province of British Columbia to the north, Idaho to the east, Oregon to the south along the Columbia River, and the Pacific Ocean to the west. Washington's geography is sharply bisected by the volcanic Cascade Mountain Range, featuring towering snow-capped peaks including Mount Rainier, Mount Adams, and Mount Saint Helens. Western Washington features a mild, temperate rainforest marine climate, the vast inland waterways of Puget Sound, and the Olympic Peninsula. This region centers around the Seattle-Tacoma metropolitan area, an international hub for commercial aerospace engineering, cloud computing, global software development, and maritime trade. In contrast, Eastern Washington lies within the rain shadow of the Cascades, characterized by the semi-arid Columbia Plateau, fertile volcanic soils, and extensive agricultural production made possible by Grand Coulee Dam irrigation, leading the nation in apple, pear, sweet cherry, and hop production.


Australia is a sovereign country comprising the mainland of the Australian continent, the island of Tasmania, and numerous smaller islands, spanning nearly three million square miles between the Indian and Pacific Oceans. It is the world's smallest continent and sixth-largest country by total area. Australia's geography is remarkably ancient and arid, with the vast interior Outback dominated by low plateaus, desert expanses, and the Great Victoria and Great Sandy Deserts. The eastern coast is bordered by the Great Dividing Range, which slopes down to fertile coastal lowlands where the majority of the population resides, centered in vibrant global cities such as Sydney, Melbourne, and Brisbane. Off the northeast coast lies the Great Barrier Reef, the world's largest coral reef system. Economically, Australia is a global leader in natural resource extraction and agricultural trade, serving as a primary global exporter of iron ore, liquefied natural gas, coal, gold, lithium, wheat, and wool, complemented by highly developed services, biomedical research, and advanced technological industries.
