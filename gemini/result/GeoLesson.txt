Virginia, officially the Commonwealth of Virginia and known as the Old Dominion, is located in the Mid-Atlantic and Southeastern regions of the United States. Spanning approximately forty-two thousand square miles, it is bordered by Maryland and Washington D.C. to the north and east across the Potomac River, the Atlantic Ocean to the east, North Carolina and Tennessee to the south, and Kentucky and West Virginia to the west. Virginia's geography is divided into five distinct physical provinces: the Tidewater coastal plain surrounding the Chesapeake Bay, featuring deep natural estuaries and barrier islands; the rolling hills of the Piedmont; the scenic Blue Ridge Mountains, crowned by Mount Rogers at five thousand seven hundred twenty-nine feet; the fertile agricultural limestone valleys of the Ridge and Valley province; and the rugged Cumberland Plateau in the far southwest. Economically, Virginia possesses a remarkably diverse economic base. The Hampton Roads region hosts the world's largest naval base and a premier Atlantic deepwater container port, while Northern Virginia represents the world's highest concentration of cloud computing data centers. These pillars are complemented by federal defense contracting, advanced aerospace manufacturing, biotechnology, and rich agricultural production including poultry, livestock, and specialty tobacco.


Canada is a sovereign North American federation comprising ten provinces and three territories, spanning nearly three point eight five million square miles from the Atlantic Ocean in the east to the Pacific Ocean in the west, and northward into the Arctic Ocean. It is the second-largest country in the world by total area. Canada's physical geography is dominated by the Canadian Shield, a vast expanse of ancient Precambrian igneous rock surrounding Hudson Bay, characterized by thin soils, boreal forests, and thousands of freshwater lakes. To the west rise the dramatic Canadian Rockies and Coast Mountains, while the interior plains form an extensive agricultural grain belt. The Saint Lawrence Lowlands and Great Lakes basin in southeastern Canada represent the country's demographic and industrial core, home to the vibrant global metropolitan centers of Toronto, Montreal, and the capital city of Ottawa. Economically, Canada is a global leader in natural resources, possessing the world's third-largest proven petroleum reserves in the Alberta oil sands, alongside immense exports of timber, potash, uranium, nickel, natural gas, and wheat, balanced by highly developed automotive, aerospace, artificial intelligence, and financial services sectors.
