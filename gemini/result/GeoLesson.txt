Virginia, officially the Commonwealth of Virginia and known as the Old Dominion, is located in the Mid-Atlantic and South Atlantic regions of the United States. Covering approximately forty-two thousand square miles, it is bordered by Maryland and Washington, D.C. to the north and east across the Potomac River, the Atlantic Ocean and Chesapeake Bay to the east, North Carolina and Tennessee to the south, and Kentucky and West Virginia to the west. Virginia's geography is defined by five distinct physical regions: the Tidewater coastal plain, characterized by deep estuaries and the world's largest natural harbor at Hampton Roads; the rolling Piedmont plateau; the Blue Ridge Mountains, home to Mount Rogers; the Valley and Ridge province, featuring the fertile Shenandoah Valley; and the Appalachian Plateau in the far southwest. Economically, Virginia possesses one of the nation's most dynamic and diverse economies. Northern Virginia hosts the world's largest concentration of cloud computing data centers, alongside extensive federal defense and technology contractors. The Hampton Roads region anchors the world's largest naval installation at Naval Station Norfolk and major commercial shipbuilding, while central and southern Virginia support robust agriculture, including poultry, beef cattle, soybeans, and wine production.


Canada is the northernmost country in North America and the second-largest country in the world by total land area, encompassing nearly three point eight five million square miles stretching from the Atlantic to the Pacific and northward into the Arctic Ocean. Canada's geography is immensely vast and varied, dominated geographically by the Canadian Shield, an ancient, rocky geological core rich in minerals, freshwater lakes, and extensive boreal forests. In the west, the Canadian Rockies present towering alpine landscapes, while the vast Interior Plains produce immense agricultural yields of wheat, canola, and livestock. Eastern Canada features the fertile Saint Lawrence Lowlands, home to the country's population core in Toronto and Montreal. Canada possesses more inland lakes than the rest of the world combined, holding roughly twenty percent of the planet's surface freshwater. Economically, Canada is a global leader in natural resources and advanced technology, ranking among the top global producers of crude petroleum, natural gas, uranium, nickel, gold, and timber, complemented by sophisticated automotive manufacturing, aerospace engineering, clean hydroelectric power generation, and cutting-edge artificial intelligence research clusters in Toronto, Montreal, and Vancouver.
