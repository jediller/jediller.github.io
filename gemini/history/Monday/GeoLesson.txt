New Hampshire, known as the Granite State, is located in the New England region of the northeastern United States. Spanning approximately nine thousand three hundred square miles, it is bordered by Maine to the east, Vermont to the west, Massachusetts to the south, and the Canadian province of Quebec to the north, with an eighteen-mile Atlantic coastline in the southeast. The geography of New Hampshire is defined by the White Mountains, a rugged northern subrange of the Appalachian Mountains that includes Mount Washington, the highest peak in the northeastern United States at six thousand two hundred eighty-eight feet, renowned for extreme weather conditions. The state's central region features the scenic Lakes Region, centered around Lake Winnipesaukee, while the southern Merrimack River valley serves as the demographic and industrial core. New Hampshire's economy thrives on high-tech manufacturing, precision machinery, electronic components, aerospace engineering, healthcare services, and outdoor tourism centered on skiing, hiking, and coastal recreation around historic Portsmouth harbor.


Switzerland is a landlocked alpine nation situated in Central Europe, bordered by Germany to the north, Austria and Liechtenstein to the east, Italy to the south, and France to the west. Covering approximately fifteen thousand nine hundred square miles, Switzerland's geography is divided into three distinct regions: the Swiss Alps in the south and east, covering sixty percent of the country; the Swiss Plateau in the center, housing the majority of the population and major cities; and the Jura Mountains in the northwest. The Swiss Alps serve as the hydrological heart of Western Europe, giving rise to major rivers including the Rhine and the Rhône. Switzerland's climate varies significantly with elevation, from Mediterranean microclimates around Lake Geneva and Ticino to glacial alpine environments. Economically, Switzerland is one of the world's most advanced and stable economies, renowned for high-value precision manufacturing, luxury horology, chemicals and pharmaceuticals, global banking and financial services in Zurich and Geneva, and premier international hospitality and alpine tourism.
