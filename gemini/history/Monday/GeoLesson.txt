Today's United States location is Nevada. Located in the Western region of the United States, Nevada is bounded by Oregon and Idaho to the north, Utah and Arizona to the east, and California to the west and south. Nevada is the seventh largest state by area but one of the most sparsely populated, with the majority of its residents concentrated in the Las Vegas and Reno metropolitan areas. Its physical geography lies almost entirely within the Great Basin, characterized by a series of north-south running mountain ranges separated by arid desert valleys, known as basin and range topography.


Nevada's arid, high-desert geography strongly influences its economy and natural resource utilization. The state is bounded on the west by the Sierra Nevada mountain range, which creates a severe rain shadow effect resulting in the driest climate of any US state. Abundant mineral deposits make Nevada the nation's leading producer of gold and silver, while geothermal energy resources and vast solar farms power its growing renewable energy sector. Extensive public federal lands, alpine lakes such as Lake Tahoe, and desert national parks drive tourism and outdoor recreation alongside its urban hospitality industry.


Today's country is Portugal. Situated on the Iberian Peninsula in Southwestern Europe, Portugal is the westernmost country of mainland Europe, bounded by the Atlantic Ocean to the west and south and sharing its entire northern and eastern land border with Spain. Portugal also includes the Atlantic archipelagos of the Azores and Madeira. Its physical geography is divided by the Tagus River, with mountainous, lush terrain in the north and rolling plains, dry plateaus, and coastal lowlands in the south.


Portugal's extensive Atlantic coastline has historically defined its maritime identity, trade economy, and culture. The fertile Tagus and Douro river valleys support extensive agriculture, wine production, and olive groves, while extensive cork oak forests make Portugal the world's leading exporter of natural cork. Its modern economy centers on international maritime trade, renewable wind and wave energy generation, technology hubs in Lisbon and Porto, and vibrant coastal tourism along the southern Algarve region.
