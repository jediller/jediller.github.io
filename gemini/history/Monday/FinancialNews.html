Market Closing Summary

United States stocks staged a powerful rally on Monday, November 23, 2026, snapping a seven-day losing streak for the S&P 500 and pulling both the S&P 500 and the Dow Jones Industrial Average back out of correction territory after Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive officer.

The Dow Jones Industrial Average surged 1,447.98 points, or 3.01 percent, to close at 49,595.31, rebounding sharply from Friday's finish of 48,147.33. Opening at 49,239.91, the blue-chip index traded between a low of 49,033.31 and a session peak of 49,810.26 on volume of 1.20 billion shares.

The S&P 500 jumped 326.78 points, or 4.73 percent, to settle at 7,237.17 compared with its prior close of 6,910.39. After opening at 7,227.41, the broad market benchmark ranged between a low of 7,152.66 and a high of 7,295.60 with 4.25 billion shares traded.

The Nasdaq Composite led the advance, soaring 1,471.83 points, or 6.51 percent, to close at 24,089.56 from Friday's close of 22,617.73. The tech-heavy index opened at 24,269.75 and moved between a session low of 23,794.96 and a high of 24,353.65 on heavy volume of 9.41 billion shares.
