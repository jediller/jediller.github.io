Movers and Shakers for Friday, November 27, 2026.
President Donald Trump made a series of pointed remarks from his Mar-a-Lago club in Palm Beach, Florida, on Thanksgiving Day, addressing economic data, severe holiday weather, and escalating tensions with Iran. Questioning the Commerce Department's upward revision of third-quarter gross domestic product growth from three point four percent to four point three percent, Trump called the pre-election figures fake and demanded an investigation, telling reporters it was unheard of in history for estimates to rise by nearly a full percentage point. On severe weather, after signing a federal disaster declaration for earlier flooding in Florida, Trump noted that his administration was reviewing storm aid requests from East Coast states battered by Thanksgiving flooding and high winds. Turning to foreign policy during Thanksgiving phone calls with United States service members, the president declared that the United States has victory at hand against Iran and announced the deployment of an additional aircraft carrier to the Middle East. He later told reporters that while he hoped Tehran would agree to a deal, he doubted Iranian leaders would accept terms, warning that the United States would definitely strike Iran's fortified underground Pickaxe Mountain facility near Natanz if military action resumes.
State leaders also weighed in on domestic policy, criminal justice, and holiday commerce ahead of the post-Thanksgiving weekend. California Governor Gavin Newsom sharply criticized President Trump's clemency record while defending California's death-sentence commutations, stating he would not take lectures on accountability from a president who used the pardon power to help wealthy friends and business associates avoid financial restitution while crime victims were left fighting to recover what was stolen from them. In New England, Massachusetts Governor Maura Healey proclaimed Friday, November 27, as Green Friday to encourage residents to support local Christmas tree farms, joined by Republican State Representative Nicholas Boldyga, who urged families to shop locally and back rural agricultural businesses. Meanwhile, West Virginia Governor Patrick Morrisey issued a Thanksgiving message to residents after announcing that S and P Global Ratings granted West Virginia its first general obligation credit rating upgrade in fifteen years.
