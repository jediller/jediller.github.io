Even more remarkably, Messner completed every single ascent without the use of supplemental bottled oxygen, fundamentally transforming the ethos and techniques of high-altitude alpine mountaineering.

Today, the historic anniversary of its founding is commemorated internationally each year on October 16 as World Food Day.
