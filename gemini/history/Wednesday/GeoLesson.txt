Situated in the Southeastern region of the United States, Tennessee is bounded by Kentucky and Virginia to the north, North Carolina to the east, Georgia, Alabama, and Mississippi to the south, and Arkansas and Missouri to the west across the Mississippi River. Tennessee features a land area of over forty-two thousand square miles and is geographically divided into three major Grand Divisions: East, Middle, and West Tennessee. Its physical terrain varies from the rugged Blue Ridge Mountains and Great Smoky Mountains along its eastern border to the rolling limestone hills of the Nashville Basin in the center and the fertile alluvial plains of the Gulf Coastal Plain along the Mississippi River in the west.

The state is drained by major river systems, including the Tennessee and Cumberland rivers, which feed extensive hydroelectric reservoirs managed by the Tennessee Valley Authority. Major urban centers include Nashville, the state capital and music industry hub; Memphis, a critical logistics port along the Mississippi River; and Knoxville and Chattanooga, key industrial and technology research corridors in the Appalachian foothills.


Today's country is Vietnam. Located on the eastern edge of the Indochina Peninsula in Southeast Asia, Vietnam borders China to the north, Laos and Cambodia to the west, and the South China Sea to the east and south. Extending over one thousand miles from north to south, Vietnam features an elongated S-shape terrain characterized by tropical lowlands, mountainous highlands, and extensive river deltas. Over three-quarters of its land area consists of hills and tropical mountains, dominated by the Annamite Range running along its western border.


Vietnam's geography is anchored by two immense, highly fertile river deltas: the Red River Delta in the north surrounding Hanoi, and the Mekong River Delta in the south surrounding Ho Chi Minh City. These agricultural river plains make Vietnam one of the world's leading exporters of rice, coffee, and seafood. Vietnam's extensive three-thousand-mile coastline features deepwater ports that drive its booming manufacturing, electronics assembly, and international maritime trade sectors.
