Today's United States location is Iowa. Located in the Midwestern region of the United States, Iowa is the only state bordered by two navigable rivers: the Mississippi River forming its entire eastern boundary and the Missouri River, along with the Big Sioux River, forming its western border. It is bordered by Minnesota to the north, Wisconsin and Illinois to the east, Missouri to the south, and Nebraska and South Dakota to the west. Covering approximately fifty-six thousand square miles, Iowa's topography consists primarily of rolling hills and prairie plains shaped by past glaciation, including the Dissected Till Plains in the south and the unique, driftless karst topography in the northeast.


Iowa's deep, fertile mollisol soils and reliable summer rainfall make it one of the most productive agricultural regions on Earth. The state leads the nation in corn, soybean, pork, and egg production, as well as renewable ethanol and wind energy generation. The Des Moines, Cedar, and Iowa rivers flow across the state, supporting major agricultural processing and industrial manufacturing centers. Des Moines, centrally located along the Des Moines River, serves as the state capital and a major international insurance and financial services center, while Cedar Rapids, Davenport, and Sioux City anchor regional commerce along major river transit corridors.


Today's country is Romania. Located in Southeastern and Central Europe, Romania is bordered by Ukraine to the north and east, Moldova to the northeast, the Black Sea to the southeast, Bulgaria to the south, Serbia to the southwest, and Hungary to the northwest. Covering approximately ninety-two thousand square miles, Romania's geography is defined by a central arc formed by the Carpathian Mountains, which encircle the high Transylvanian Plateau and divide it from the fertile plains of Walachia in the south and Moldavia in the east.


Romania's southern border is largely formed by the Danube River, which flows into the Black Sea, creating the Danube Delta, one of Europe's largest, best-preserved wetland ecosystems and a vital international biosphere reserve. The country possesses rich natural resources, including fertile agricultural soils for grain production, vast timber reserves in the Carpathians, and historic petroleum and natural gas deposits. The capital city of Bucharest, located on the southern Romanian Plain, serves as a dynamic commercial, industrial, and software technology center, while the port city of Constanța on the Black Sea operates as one of the largest container and bulk cargo ports in Europe.
