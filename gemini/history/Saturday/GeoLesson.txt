Located in the Midwestern and Great Lakes regions of the United States, Indiana is bordered by Lake Michigan and the state of Michigan to the north, Ohio to the east, Kentucky across the Ohio River to the south, and Illinois to the west across the Wabash River and land boundary. Located in Northwestern Europe, Ireland is an island nation situated in the North Atlantic Ocean, separated from Great Britain by the Irish Sea, the North Channel, and Saint George's Channel. Covering over twenty-seven thousand square miles, the Republic of Ireland occupies the southern and central five-sixths of the island. Ireland's physical geography is characterized by a central lowland plain of fertile pastures, bogs, and lakes, surrounded by a rugged coastal rim of low mountain ranges including the MacGillycuddy's Reeks, the Wicklow Mountains, and the dramatic sea cliffs of the Atlantic western coast.


Ireland's maritime climate, moderated by the North Atlantic Current, provides mild temperatures and abundant rainfall throughout the year, sustaining lush green pastures that give Ireland its nickname, the Emerald Isle. This geography supports a world-renowned dairy and livestock agricultural sector. Dublin, situated on the east coast at the mouth of the River Liffey, serves as the nation's capital and a leading European financial, pharmaceutical, and technology hub. Ireland's extensive Atlantic coastline also provides strategic marine resources, renewable offshore wind energy potential, and deepwater maritime shipping connections.
